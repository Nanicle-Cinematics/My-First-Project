Security & Trust

Your church data stays yours — private, backed up, and secure.

🏛

Private deployment

Your church gets its own server — your data is never shared with other organisations. You can request a full export at any time.

🔐

Two-factor authentication

Admins can enable 2FA (authenticator app). Recovery codes ensure no one gets permanently locked out.

📋

Audit log

Every sensitive action — login, deletion, bulk SMS — is logged with timestamp and user. Full accountability.

💾

Nightly backups

Automated backups every night. Quarterly backup files emailed directly to the church treasurer on Pro and Enterprise plans.

🩺

Always-on uptime

Health monitoring, auto-restart, and at least one machine always running ensures the system is available when you need it.

🔒

HTTPS everywhere

All traffic encrypted in transit. Passwords hashed with bcrypt. No plain-text credentials stored anywhere.
