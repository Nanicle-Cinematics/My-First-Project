Frequently asked questions

Who owns the data?

You do — completely. The data is yours; we're just the caretaker. You can download a full backup of your database any time, and we'll hand it over if you ever stop using the system.

Is the data secure?

Yes. Each church gets its own isolated database — no shared storage with other churches. All connections use HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Nightly off-site backups. We never sell or share your member information.

Do we need to install anything?

No. The system runs in any modern web browser — phone, tablet, laptop, or desktop. There's nothing to install or update.

How do the SMS credits work?

Each plan includes a monthly SMS allowance. Extra messages are billed at GH₵ 0.025 each (the same as Arkesel's rate — we don't mark it up). A long message over 160 characters counts as multiple SMS.

Can we use our own Arkesel / email account?

Yes — on any plan, you can connect your own Arkesel API key and Gmail account. Then you pay Arkesel directly, with no per-message fee from us.

What about Mobile Money giving?

Coming soon — MTN MoMo, Vodafone Cash, and AirtelTigo Money via Hubtel / Paystack. Members will tap a link from a broadcast and pay through their wallet; the system auto-records the tithe or special offering.

Can we move our existing member list over?

Yes. Export from your current system (or even from a Word document or WhatsApp list), send us the file, and we'll import it into your installation for free on the Pro and Annual plans.

What if we want to cancel?

Cancel any time — we'll send you a full backup of your data and that's it. No long-term contracts, no cancellation fees.
